## Intent

A credible hackathon pitch film for StockPulse that tells one real purchasing problem end to end, followed separately by a live demo. Style: ethereal, utilitarian, fresh enterprise UI, Swiss editorial minimalism, subtle cinematic motion, matching the app. Real app footage anchors credibility; motion explains how the pieces connect; restrained audio adds polish.

- ../../logo_image/logo_icon.png — app icon, small uses only.
- assets/voiceover/VO_01_HOOK.mp3 … VO_12_CLOSE.mp3 — ElevenLabs narration, supplied by the user after approval.

## Customizations

- 1920×1080, true 60 fps, H.264 MP4, stereo 48 kHz, readable captions (SRT plus burned-in).
- Playwright captures of the real app; HyperFrames for the composition; FFmpeg for the mix, loudness, captions, and encode. No Remotion.
- Blue = deterministic, ember = AI, evergreen = human approval; present-tense "working product" voice per the planning docs; AI steps as motion graphics; one disclosure line in S11; footage never altered.
- Original synthesized music bed and SFX (no third-party audio).

## Notes

- Backend and AI models are not built or connected yet (planned); film shows the prototype UI on sample data and labels backend/AI as PLANNED.
- Hard maximum 3:45. Never fake API success, GPU inference, solver runs, dispatch, or approvals. No invented metrics, testimonials, neon, dark-mode dominance, robots/brains, orbs, or stock footage.
- Storyboard gate: stop after pre-production for approval.
